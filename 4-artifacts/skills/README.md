# Skills

**Capabilities your daimon applies** — what it *knows how to do*, often mid-flow without a special trigger.
Examples: research a company, write in your voice, design a plan. Each packaged as a `SKILL.md`.

**The trio — so you don't mix them up:**
- **Skill** *(here)* — *what it knows how to do.* A capability it applies. → `4-artifacts/skills/`
- **Command** — *how you call it.* A verb you invoke. → `3-actions/commands/`
- **Tool** — *what it can reach.* An external integration. → `4-artifacts/tools/`

Add as you grow.
